## Understanding Color Mechanics

Your chameleon can cycle through six primary colors: red, blue, yellow, green, orange, and purple. Each transformation takes 0.5 seconds to complete, during which you remain vulnerable to environmental hazards. The color wheel follows a specific sequence that becomes crucial for advanced play.

Color matching occurs when your chameleon touches objects, platforms, or collectibles of the same hue. Successful matches trigger particle effects and award points based on your current multiplier, which ranges from 1x to 10x depending on consecutive successful matches.
