## Optimal Purchase Timing Strategies

### Best Times to Buy

**Steam Summer Sale (Late June)** represents the most reliable annual discount window, typically running for 10-14 days with the full 40% reduction. This sale consistently offers the deepest available discount alongside extensive visibility through Steam's featured promotions.

**Winter Sale (Mid-December through Early January)** provides another guaranteed discount period, often coinciding with holiday gift-giving seasons. The extended 2-week duration offers flexibility for purchase timing within the sale window.

### Secondary Opportunities

Spring and autumn sales offer **less predictable but frequent alternatives**, with discounts ranging from 25-40%. ConcernedApe occasionally runs independent publisher sales outside major Steam events, though these typically match rather than exceed seasonal sale pricing.
