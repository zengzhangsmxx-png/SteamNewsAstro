## Network Issues Resolved After Three-Week Outage Period

FromSoftware has successfully restored ELDEN RING's multiplayer infrastructure following widespread connection problems that plagued the game from mid-May through early June 2026. The developer implemented comprehensive server fixes in patch 1.12.3, released June 5th, which addressed critical network stability issues affecting co-op summoning, PvP invasions, and message system functionality. Player reports indicate multiplayer sessions now maintain consistent connectivity, with connection success rates improving from approximately 30% during the outage period to 85-90% post-patch.

The network problems initially surfaced on May 18th, when players across all platforms began experiencing frequent disconnections during multiplayer sessions. Steam discussion forums documented over 15,000 complaint posts within the first week, with similar issues reported on PlayStation and Xbox platforms. FromSoftware acknowledged the problems on May 22nd through their official Twitter account, citing "unexpected server load distribution issues" as the primary cause.

## Current Multiplayer Population Analysis

Recent data from Steam's API indicates ELDEN RING maintains a healthy multiplayer ecosystem following the network restoration. During peak evening hours (6-10 PM local time), approximately 15-20% of active players engage in multiplayer activities, translating to 45,000-65,000 concurrent multiplayer participants globally.

The Steam platform continues to host the largest multiplayer community, with PC players showing higher engagement rates in both co-op and PvP activities. PlayStation 5 users represent the second-largest group, while Xbox Series players maintain steady but smaller participation numbers.

Co-op summoning experiences have notably improved since the patch deployment. Players report successful summon sign placement rates of 85-90%, compared to the 30-40% success rates experienced during the outage period. Boss fight areas show the highest co-op activity, with locations like Malenia's arena, Radagon's chamber, and the Fire Giant encounter maintaining consistent summon availability throughout most hours.

## PvP and Invasion Activity Status

The invasion system has returned to pre-outage functionality levels, with dedicated PvP players reporting stable connections and reduced latency issues. Popular invasion hotspots including Caelid's Dragonbarrow, Limgrave's Gatefront Ruins, and Liurnia's Academy Gate maintain active invasion pools during prime gaming hours.

PvP tournament organizers have resumed scheduled events after suspending activities during the network instability period. The community-run "Tarnished Champions League" announced its return for June 15th, expecting participation from approximately 500-800 players across multiple skill divisions.

FromSoftware indicated in their June 7th developer update that additional server optimization work continues for regions showing lower performance metrics.

## Message System and Community Features

The player message system has regained full functionality, with bloodstains, phantom appearances, and player-left messages displaying consistently across all game areas. This restoration addresses one of the most frequently cited concerns during the outage, as many players rely on community messages for guidance and area warnings.

Message appraisal rates have returned to normal levels, with popular messages in high-traffic areas receiving 500-2,000 appraisals daily. The restoration of this system particularly benefits new players navigating challenging areas like Caelid or the Haligtree.

## Developer Response and Future Improvements

FromSoftware president Hidetaka Miyazaki addressed the network issues in a June 8th statement, acknowledging player frustration and outlining planned infrastructure improvements. The development team committed to implementing redundant server architecture to prevent similar widespread outages in future updates.

The company allocated additional resources toward multiplayer infrastructure monitoring, establishing 24/7 network status tracking across all supported regions. This monitoring system aims to identify and resolve connection issues within hours rather than the weeks-long resolution period experienced in May.

## Current Recommendations for Players

Players seeking optimal multiplayer experiences should connect during regional peak hours when player populations reach maximum density. Co-op assistance remains most readily available for endgame bosses and challenging optional encounters, with response times averaging 30-90 seconds during busy periods.

For PvP enthusiasts, invasion activity peaks during evening hours in each respective region. Players in Southeast Asia and South America may experience occasional connection hiccups, but these issues typically resolve within individual session attempts rather than preventing extended gameplay periods.

The current network stability represents a significant improvement over the May outage period, restoring ELDEN RING's multiplayer ecosystem to functional levels that support both casual co-op play and competitive PvP engagement across all supported platforms.
