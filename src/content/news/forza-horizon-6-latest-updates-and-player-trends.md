## Forza Horizon 6 Season 2 Delivers Major Mexico Expansion

Playground Games released Forza Horizon 6's Season 2 update on May 28, 2026, introducing the Mexico expansion that adds three distinct biomes spanning 140 square kilometers of new racing territory. The update includes 47 new vehicles, cross-platform multiplayer functionality, and enhanced online racing modes that have maintained strong player engagement across all platforms. Steam concurrent player counts have remained stable between 240,000-280,000 daily peaks, according to SteamDB tracking data, with weekend event participation pushing numbers above 320,000 players.

The Mexico expansion represents the largest content addition since the game's February 2026 launch, featuring desert canyons, tropical coastal roads, and mountainous terrain that leverages the updated weather system introduced in patch 1.4.2. Developer livestreams on May 15 confirmed that the expansion required 18 months of development and incorporates photogrammetry data from 12 real-world Mexican locations.

## Cross-Platform Integration and Multiplayer Enhancements

Season 2's marquee feature enables seamless cross-platform racing between Steam, Xbox Game Pass PC, and Xbox Series X|S consoles. Playground Games technical director Sarah Chen announced during the May 20 developer update that cross-platform lobbies support up to 72 players simultaneously, representing a 20% increase from the previous 60-player limit. The implementation uses Xbox Live backend infrastructure to maintain consistent matchmaking performance across platforms.

Player clubs can now recruit members regardless of platform, with shared progression tracking and leaderboards. The update also introduces ranked competitive seasons lasting 6 weeks each, featuring exclusive vehicle rewards and cosmetic unlocks tied to performance ratings. Initial participation data shows 78% of active players have engaged with competitive modes within the first week of launch.

## Technical Performance and Known Issues

The Season 2 update addresses 73 reported bugs from the community feedback portal, including steering wheel calibration improvements and HDR display optimization for ultrawide monitors. However, some players continue experiencing intermittent crashes during convoy formation, particularly when transitioning between Mexico and the original Australian map regions.

Playground Games released hotfix 2.1.1 on June 4 to resolve texture streaming issues affecting players with 8GB VRAM graphics cards. The developer recommends 12GB VRAM for optimal experience with the Mexico expansion's enhanced environmental details enabled at ultra settings.
