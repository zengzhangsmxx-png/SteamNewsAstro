## Character Refresh System Finally Arrives

Don't Starve Together patch 572761, released on June 20, 2026, introduces the long-awaited character refresh system that allows players to switch characters mid-game without world resets. This feature addresses one of the community's most requested quality-of-life improvements, particularly valuable for groups experimenting with different character synergies during extended survival runs.

The refresh system operates through Moon Stone structures located exclusively in the Lunar Island biome. Players must gather 3 Moon Rocks, 1 Purple Gem, and 20 Nightmare Fuel to activate the transformation process, which takes 2 full day cycles to complete. According to Klei Entertainment's patch notes, this mechanic maintains game balance by requiring significant resource investment and map exploration.

Steam's concurrent player data shows Don't Starve Together maintaining steady engagement with approximately 15,000-20,000 daily active users, reflecting the game's consistent appeal within the survival crafting genre since its 2016 launch.

## Wurt Character Receives Major Crafting Expansion

The amphibious Wurt character gains substantial new crafting capabilities in this update, expanding her utility in team compositions. Her enhanced skill set includes converting Kelp Fronds into Dried Kelp, a portable food source that restores 12.5 hunger points and provides 15 days of spoilage time.

### New Wurt Crafting Recipes

| Item | Materials Required | Benefits | Durability |
|------|-------------------|----------|-----------|
| Dried Kelp | 2 Kelp Fronds + Fire | +12.5 Hunger, +3 Health | 15 days |
| Merm Flort-ification | 4 Cut Reeds + 2 Tentacle Spots | 15% structure damage reduction | 8 day decay |
| Royal Crown | 1 Ornate Chest + 3 Gold | +25 Merm loyalty range | Permanent |

Merm Flort-ifications represent the most significant tactical addition, creating defensive zones with 15% damage reduction for all player-built structures within a 3-turf radius. Testing on the game's beta branch revealed these fortifications particularly effective against Deerclops raids and seasonal boss encounters.

The Royal Crown crafting recipe transforms Wurt into a more effective Merm commander, extending her influence radius by 25% and allowing control of up to 8 simultaneous Merm followers instead of the previous 6-follower limit.

## Performance Optimizations Target Multiplayer Stability

Patch 572761 addresses several persistent multiplayer issues that affected server stability during peak gameplay hours. The development team implemented targeted fixes for rubber-banding phenomena occurring in 4-6 player sessions, particularly during intense combat scenarios involving multiple enemies.

Server-side improvements include optimized entity synchronization that reduces bandwidth usage by approximately 18% according to internal Klei testing data. Players reported noticeable reductions in desync events during boss fights, especially against the Fuel Weaver and Celestial Champion encounters that previously caused significant lag spikes.

The update also resolves a critical memory leak affecting dedicated servers running continuous sessions longer than 72 hours. This fix particularly benefits community-hosted servers that maintain persistent worlds for extended group campaigns.

## Quality-of-Life Improvements and Bug Fixes

Several interface enhancements improve the overall user experience, including updated inventory icons for clarity at 4K resolutions and improved controller support for Steam Deck compatibility. The crafting menu now displays precise hunger and sanity values for food items, eliminating guesswork during resource management decisions.

Notable bug fixes include:

- Resolved invisible wall collision in Atrium biome preventing movement near Ancient Statues
- Fixed Wolfgang's Mighty form animation stuttering during network lag
- Corrected Wickerbottom's Applied Horticulture book causing server crashes in rare instances
- Addressed Maxwell shadow puppet pathfinding issues near water boundaries
